Deercast — privacy

Deercast is a private hunting app used by a small group of invited people. This page explains what it stores. Last updated November 10, 2026.

Signing in

You sign in with Google. Deercast receives only your name, email address and profile picture from Google — nothing else in your Google account. Only people the app owner has added can see anything in the app.

What is stored

What you enter yourself: hunting properties you're a member of, stands and map marks, hunt logs, deer sightings, shots and tracking paths (including GPS locations you record while tracking), and your bow and gun setups. It is stored in a private database (Supabase), protected so that each person sees only their own records and the properties they've been added to.

Location

Your phone's location is used only when you ask for it (for example "use where I am now", or while a tracking map is recording your path) and only what you save is kept.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers. Weather, maps and county lookups are fetched from public services (Open-Meteo, Esri, OpenStreetMap, US Census, FCC) using map coordinates only, never your name or email. There are no ads and no tracking cookies.

Deleting your data

Ask the app owner to remove your account and everything you've entered; it will be deleted from the database and from backups as they roll over.

Contact

Questions: contact the app owner through the email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
